how to play
THREE RULES
  1. Four stones to a line. Every row and every column holds exactly four stones and three empties.
  2. None touching its equal. No two stones side by side may share a weight.
  3. The edge numbers are the sum. Each clue is the total weight of its line.

A load gets ruled through once its line is finished — four stones, the sum met, nothing touching its equal. Take a stone back and the rule lifts again.

Bedrock stones — ringed in oxide, with an age — came from the boards you already solved. They are locked.

A cell you have ruled out takes an ×. Not sure yet? NOTES pencils the weights that are still possible along the bottom of a cell.
